How this works: Every ADT message processed by the Mirth endpoint generates an 837 claim and a simulated 835 ERA response. The reconciliation math is: balance_due = billed − paid − patient_responsibility. A non-zero balance_due means the provider is owed money that wasn't covered by the payer or the patient. In production, the 835 arrives separately from the clearinghouse and is matched to the 837 by claim ID.
